Guest Operations approvals

This page is reserved for exact-request approvals of Guest Operations development and service actions.

Approval service not connected.

Pending requests and Approve / Reject actions are unavailable until a separately authenticated, audited Guest Operations approval API is installed. No approval can be granted from this page yet.

Required before activation

The integration must verify the owner identity on the server, list only authorized pending proposals, display the exact action and expiry, require an explicit decision, and record the decision against the immutable proposal ID and state. It must reject expired or changed proposals and must not execute a task merely by viewing this page.

Community and Guest Operations are separate security domains. This page intentionally does not access host files, credentials or approval databases directly.
