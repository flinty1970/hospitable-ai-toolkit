Webhook connection

 
The local test checks authentication and the handler without queuing a guest message. It does not check public DNS, HTTPS, firewalls or Hospitable delivery. Receipt times describe authenticated requests; the toolkit does not independently verify Hospitable's identity with a signature.

Set up Hospitable webhooks
  1. Make the toolkit webhook reachable through a public HTTPS hostname. Follow the Caddy instructions in the README. Keep owner pages restricted to your LAN/VPN.
  2. Enter that HTTPS origin below and reveal the private destination URL.
  3. In Hospitable, go to Apps / Integrations → Webhooks → Add new. Choose the v2 Messages webhook, paste the destination URL and save.
  4. Keep toolkit response modes Paused or Draft only while testing. Hospitable's Test button can replay a real message; the toolkit will deduplicate known messages, but a previously unseen replay may be processed.
  5. Click Test in Hospitable, then Refresh receipt status here. Confirm a new receipt time. A real pre-booking inquiry still needs its own end-to-end test.
